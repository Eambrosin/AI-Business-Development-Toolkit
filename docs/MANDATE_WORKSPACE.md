# Ambrosin Commercial Mandate Workspace

The **Ambrosin Commercial Mandate Workspace** is the shared navigation and context layer across the Commercial Intelligence portfolio.

It does not replace the four applications. It connects them around one commercial project.

## Operating principle

```text
COMMERCIAL MANDATE
        ↓
IDENTIFY
Market Entry & Opportunity Intelligence
        ↓
PRIORITIZE and/or PARTNER
Counterparty Qualification / Route-to-Market Intelligence
        ↓
ENGAGE
Business Development Execution
        ↓
Opportunity → Deal Sheet → Mandate Reporting
```

## Shared mandate context

The same mandate can carry:

- represented company / client
- origin → target market corridor
- product / solution
- commercial objective
- buyer / partner focus
- target buyer types
- target partner types
- route-to-market hypothesis
- value proposition
- known constraints
- HS / CN / NCM references when available
- target territories
- working languages
- success criteria

The mandate is portable as JSON and can be loaded independently in any shipped application.

## Workspace navigation

Each live application exposes the same four operational modules:

### IDENTIFY
Discover and screen buyer, route-to-market and execution-partner candidates while preserving public evidence and trade-feasibility questions.

### PRIORITIZE
Qualify and prioritize counterparties against the commercial mandate using explainable scoring and explicit evidence gaps.

### PARTNER
Evaluate route-to-market partners using strategic fit plus execution readiness, including technical service, after-sales, installation / commissioning, spare-parts, field-service and industrial-reference capability.

### ENGAGE
Execute outreach and follow-up, record responses and meetings, progress active opportunities, structure Commercial Deal Sheets and report mandate activity.

## Progression logic

The workspace recommends the next module based on the current stage and mandate focus:

- IDENTIFY + Buyers → PRIORITIZE
- IDENTIFY + Partners → PARTNER
- IDENTIFY + Buyers + Partners → PRIORITIZE / PARTNER
- PRIORITIZE → ENGAGE
- PARTNER → ENGAGE
- ENGAGE → execution, opportunity management and reporting

This recommendation is workflow guidance, not an automated commercial decision.

## Evidence discipline

The workspace preserves the portfolio principles:

- unknown remains unknown
- research completeness is not purchase intent
- a partner candidate is not automatically an executable partner
- trade-feasibility states are not legal, customs or technical conclusions
- internal scores are not presented externally as buyer intent
- commercial execution remains human-led

## Purpose

The workspace supports the Ambrosin Legal Trade operating model:

```text
Market Intelligence
        ↓
Business Development
        ↓
Commercial Execution
        ↓
Reporting / Next Action
```

The objective is to preserve commercial reasoning, evidence and next actions across the full mandate rather than treating research, qualification, partner development and outreach as disconnected tasks.
