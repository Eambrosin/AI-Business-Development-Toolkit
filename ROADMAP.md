# AI Business Development Toolkit — Roadmap

The portfolio currently has four shipped Commercial Intelligence applications:

```text
ACCOUNT DEVELOPMENT
IDENTIFY → PRIORITIZE → ENGAGE

PARTNERSHIP DEVELOPMENT
IDENTIFY / Partner Universe → PARTNER → ENGAGE
```

The roadmap is intentionally narrow. New modules are not presented as shipped products until they reach the same implementation, testing and demo standard as the current applications.

## Current Portfolio Priorities

The current priority is **integration and reliability**, not adding more standalone products.

Focus areas:

- consistent terminology across applications
- stable portable handoffs
- shared market-profile conventions
- transparent evidence states
- stronger cross-product interoperability
- clearer portfolio documentation
- recruiter- and stakeholder-friendly demos
- production-quality testing and secret scanning

---

## Roadmap — Market Expansion Intelligence

**Status:** Roadmap / not shipped

The future Market Expansion Intelligence layer is intended to support structured market-entry decisions.

Potential decision areas include:

- market attractiveness
- commercial fit
- route-to-market options
- customer / account availability
- partnership requirements
- localization needs
- execution complexity
- regulatory and operational validation
- commercial risks
- 90-day entry planning

The design principle is the same as the rest of the portfolio:

```text
EVIDENCE
    ↓
COMMERCIAL INTERPRETATION
    ↓
EXPLAINABLE DECISION LOGIC
    ↓
VALIDATION QUESTIONS
    ↓
NEXT ACTION
```

It will remain a roadmap item until there is an implemented application with:

- a defined commercial workflow
- test-covered decision logic
- clear limitations
- a usable live demo
- evidence-aware outputs
- documented integration with the rest of the portfolio

---

## Not a Current Priority

The portfolio does not currently need additional standalone concepts simply to increase repository count.

Ideas such as:

- B2G-specific intelligence
- cross-border readiness
- unified executive dashboards
- deeper CRM integrations

may be explored later only when they solve a clear commercial problem and add more value than complexity.

---

## Architecture Principle

The long-term objective is not a larger collection of apps.

It is a coherent Commercial Intelligence system where each layer answers a distinct question:

```text
IDENTIFY
What deserves investigation?

PRIORITIZE
Where should commercial attention go?

ENGAGE
How should we approach the opportunity?

PARTNER
Which strategic relationships create leverage?

MARKET EXPANSION — ROADMAP
How should a new market be validated and entered?
```
